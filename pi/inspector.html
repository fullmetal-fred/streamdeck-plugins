Tips for remote consoles

Slow KVM/iLO? Increase Keystroke Delay to 50–100ms. Some consoles need 100ms+ to keep up.

Start Delay gives you time to click into the remote console window after pressing the Stream Deck button.

Max Characters prevents accidental mega-pastes. Increase it if you regularly paste large configs.
